Questions

Do I need to know any Arabic to start?
No. Plenty of students start with the letters and nothing else. The teacher will find your level in the first session and build from there.
How do the lessons actually happen?
Live video call at the time you booked. You only need a phone, tablet or laptop with a camera and a steady connection.
What age do you teach?
Children from around five years old, and adults of any age. The teaching style changes to match.
What if we need to miss a lesson?
Let your teacher know ahead of time and the lesson gets moved rather than lost.
How do I pay?
Monthly, and we mostly take PayPal. If you cannot use PayPal or need an alternative arrangement, just contact us and we will work something out.
