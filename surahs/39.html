Tajweed keyrules in this surah
  • Madd Laazimمد لازمHold 6 counts. Obligatory.
  • Madd Muttasilمد متصلHold 4 to 5 counts.
  • Madd Munfasilمد منفصلHold 4 to 5 counts.
  • Madd al-Aaridمد عارضHold 2, 4 or 6 counts when stopping.
  • IqlabإقلابNoon turns into a meem sound.
  • Idghaam w/ Ghunnahإدغام بغنةMerge into the next letter with nasalisation.
  • Idghaam w/o Ghunnahإدغام بغير غنةMerge into the next letter, no nasalisation.
  • Idghaam Shafawiإدغام شفويMeem merges into meem.
  • Ikhfa Shafawiإخفاء شفويHide the meem before a baa.
  • IkhfaإخفاءHide the noon with a light nasal sound.
  • GhunnahغنةNasal sound, 2 counts.
  • QalqalahقلقلةEcho the letter with a slight bounce.
  • Lam Shamsiyyahلام شمسيةSilent laam, merged into the next letter.
  • Hamzat al-Waslهمزة وصلSkipped when joined to the word before.
  • Silent letterحرف لا ينطقWritten but not pronounced.
  • Madd Tabee'iمد طبيعيNatural madd. Hold 2 counts.

جَهَنَّمَjahannama زُمَرًاۖzumaran حَتَّىٰٓhattaa إِذَاidhaa جَآءُوهَاjaa'oohaa فُتِحَتْfutihat أَبْوَٰبُهَاabwaabuhaa وَقَالَwaqaala لَهُمْlahum خَزَنَتُهَآkhazanatuhaa أَلَمْalam يَأْتِكُمْya'tikum رُسُلٌrusulun مِّنكُمْminkum يَتْلُونَyatloona عَلَيْكُمْ'alaykum ءَايَٰتِaayaati رَبِّكُمْrabbikum وَيُنذِرُونَكُمْwayundhiroonakum لِقَآءَliqaa'a يَوْمِكُمْyawmikum هَٰذَاۚhaadhaa قَالُواْqaalooaa بَلَىٰbalaa وَلَٰكِنْwalaakin حَقَّتْhaqqat كَلِمَةُkalimahu ٱلْعَذَابِl-'adhaabi عَلَى'alaa ٱلْكَٰفِرِينَl-kaafireen٧١ قِيلَqeela ٱدْخُلُوٓاْd-khulooaa أَبْوَٰبَabwaaba جَهَنَّمَjahannama خَٰلِدِينَkhaalideena فِيهَاۖfeehaa فَبِئْسَfabi'sa مَثْوَىmathwaa ٱلْمُتَكَبِّرِينَl-mutakabbireen٧٢ وَسِيقَwaseeqa ٱلَّذِينَl-ladheena ٱتَّقَوْاْt-taqawaa رَبَّهُمْrabbahum إِلَىilaa ٱلْجَنَّةِl-jannahi زُمَرًاۖzumaran حَتَّىٰٓhattaa إِذَاidhaa جَآءُوهَاjaa'oohaa وَفُتِحَتْwafutihat أَبْوَٰبُهَاabwaabuhaa وَقَالَwaqaala لَهُمْlahum خَزَنَتُهَاkhazanatuhaa سَلَٰمٌsalaamun عَلَيْكُمْ'alaykum طِبْتُمْtibtum فَٱدْخُلُوهَاfadkhuloohaa خَٰلِدِينَkhaalideen٧٣ وَقَالُواْwaqaalooaa ٱلْحَمْدُl-hamdu لِلَّهِlillaahi ٱلَّذِيl-ladhee صَدَقَنَاsadaqanaa وَعْدَهُۥwa'dahoo وَأَوْرَثَنَاwa'awrathanaa ٱلْأَرْضَl-'arda نَتَبَوَّأُnatabawwa'u مِنَmina ٱلْجَنَّةِl-jannahi حَيْثُhaythu نَشَآءُۖnashaa'u فَنِعْمَfani'ma أَجْرُajru ٱلْعَٰمِلِينَl-'aamileen٧٤ وَتَرَىwataraa ٱلْمَلَٰٓئِكَةَl-malaa'ikaha حَآفِّينَhaaffeena مِنْmin حَوْلِhawli ٱلْعَرْشِl-'arshi يُسَبِّحُونَyusabbihoona بِحَمْدِbihamdi رَبِّهِمْۚrabbihim وَقُضِيَwaqudiya بَيْنَهُمbaynahum بِٱلْحَقِّۚbilhaqqi وَقِيلَwaqeela ٱلْحَمْدُl-hamdu لِلَّهِlillaahi رَبِّrabbi ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَl-'aalameen٧٥
