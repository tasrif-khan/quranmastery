Tajweed keyrules in this surah
  • Madd Laazimمد لازمHold 6 counts. Obligatory.
  • Madd Muttasilمد متصلHold 4 to 5 counts.
  • Madd Munfasilمد منفصلHold 4 to 5 counts.
  • Madd al-Aaridمد عارضHold 2, 4 or 6 counts when stopping.
  • IqlabإقلابNoon turns into a meem sound.
  • Idghaam w/ Ghunnahإدغام بغنةMerge into the next letter with nasalisation.
  • Idghaam w/o Ghunnahإدغام بغير غنةMerge into the next letter, no nasalisation.
  • Idghaam Shafawiإدغام شفويMeem merges into meem.
  • Idghaam Mutajanisaynإدغام متجانسينLetters of the same articulation merge.
  • Ikhfa Shafawiإخفاء شفويHide the meem before a baa.
  • IkhfaإخفاءHide the noon with a light nasal sound.
  • GhunnahغنةNasal sound, 2 counts.
  • QalqalahقلقلةEcho the letter with a slight bounce.
  • Lam Shamsiyyahلام شمسيةSilent laam, merged into the next letter.
  • Hamzat al-Waslهمزة وصلSkipped when joined to the word before.
  • Silent letterحرف لا ينطقWritten but not pronounced.
  • Madd Tabee'iمد طبيعيNatural madd. Hold 2 counts.

ٱللَّهِl-laahi ٱلَّذِيٓl-ladhee أَتْقَنَatqana كُلَّkulla شَيْءٍۚshay'in إِنَّهُۥinnahoo خَبِيرُۢkhabeeru بِمَاbimaa تَفْعَلُونَtaf'aloon٨٨ مَنman جَآءَjaa'a بِٱلْحَسَنَةِbilhasanahi فَلَهُۥfalahoo خَيْرٌkhayrun مِّنْهَاminhaa وَهُمwahum مِّنmin فَزَعٍfaza'in يَوْمَئِذٍyawma'idhin ءَامِنُونَaaminoon٨٩ وَمَنwaman جَآءَjaa'a بِٱلسَّيِّئَةِbissayyi'ahi فَكُبَّتْfakubbat وُجُوهُهُمْwujoohuhum فِيfee ٱلنَّارِn-naari هَلْhal تُجْزَوْنَtujzawna إِلَّاillaa مَاmaa كُنتُمْkuntum تَعْمَلُونَta'maloon٩٠ إِنَّمَآinnamaa أُمِرْتُumirtu أَنْan أَعْبُدَa'buda رَبَّrabba هَٰذِهِhaadhihi ٱلْبَلْدَةِl-baldahi ٱلَّذِيl-ladhee حَرَّمَهَاharramahaa وَلَهُۥwalahoo كُلُّkullu شَيْءٍۖshay'in وَأُمِرْتُwa'umirtu أَنْan أَكُونَakoona مِنَmina ٱلْمُسْلِمِينَl-muslimeen٩١ وَأَنْwa'an أَتْلُوَاْatluwaa ٱلْقُرْءَانَۖl-qur'aana فَمَنِfamani ٱهْتَدَىٰh-tadaa فَإِنَّمَاfa'innamaa يَهْتَدِيyahtadee لِنَفْسِهِۦۖlinafsihee وَمَنwaman ضَلَّdalla فَقُلْfaqul إِنَّمَآinnamaa أَنَا۠anaa مِنَmina ٱلْمُنذِرِينَl-mundhireen٩٢ وَقُلِwaquli ٱلْحَمْدُl-hamdu لِلَّهِlillaahi سَيُرِيكُمْsayureekum ءَايَٰتِهِۦaayaatihee فَتَعْرِفُونَهَاۚfata'rifoonahaa وَمَاwamaa رَبُّكَrabbuka بِغَٰفِلٍbighaafilin عَمَّا'ammaa تَعْمَلُونَta'maloon٩٣
