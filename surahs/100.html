Tajweed keyrules in this surah
  • Madd al-Aaridمد عارضHold 2, 4 or 6 counts when stopping.
  • IkhfaإخفاءHide the noon with a light nasal sound.
  • GhunnahغنةNasal sound, 2 counts.
  • QalqalahقلقلةEcho the letter with a slight bounce.
  • Lam Shamsiyyahلام شمسيةSilent laam, merged into the next letter.
  • Hamzat al-Waslهمزة وصلSkipped when joined to the word before.
  • Madd Tabee'iمد طبيعيNatural madd. Hold 2 counts.
Al-'Adiyat العاديات Meccan · 11 ayat
وَٱلْعَٰدِيَٰتِwal'aadiyaati ضَبْحًاdabhaa١ فَٱلْمُورِيَٰتِfalmooriyaati قَدْحًاqadhaa٢ فَٱلْمُغِيرَٰتِfalmugheeraati صُبْحًاsubhaa٣ فَأَثَرْنَfa'atharna بِهِۦbihee نَقْعًاnaq'aa٤ فَوَسَطْنَfawasatna بِهِۦbihee جَمْعًاjam'aa٥ إِنَّinna ٱلْإِنسَٰنَl-'insaana لِرَبِّهِۦlirabbihee لَكَنُودٌlakanood٦ وَإِنَّهُۥwa'innahoo عَلَىٰ'alaa ذَٰلِكَdhaalika لَشَهِيدٌlashaheed٧ وَإِنَّهُۥwa'innahoo لِحُبِّlihubbi ٱلْخَيْرِl-khayri لَشَدِيدٌlashadeed٨ ۞أَفَلَا'afalaa يَعْلَمُya'lamu إِذَاidhaa بُعْثِرَbu'thira مَاmaa فِيfee ٱلْقُبُورِl-quboor٩ وَحُصِّلَwahussila مَاmaa فِيfee ٱلصُّدُورِs-sudoor١٠ إِنَّinna رَبَّهُمrabbahum بِهِمْbihim يَوْمَئِذٍyawma'idhin لَّخَبِيرُۢlakhabeer١١
