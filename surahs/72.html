Tajweed keyrules in this surah
  • Madd Laazimمد لازمHold 6 counts. Obligatory.
  • Madd Muttasilمد متصلHold 4 to 5 counts.
  • Madd Munfasilمد منفصلHold 4 to 5 counts.
  • IqlabإقلابNoon turns into a meem sound.
  • Idghaam w/ Ghunnahإدغام بغنةMerge into the next letter with nasalisation.
  • Idghaam w/o Ghunnahإدغام بغير غنةMerge into the next letter, no nasalisation.
  • Idghaam Shafawiإدغام شفويMeem merges into meem.
  • IkhfaإخفاءHide the noon with a light nasal sound.
  • GhunnahغنةNasal sound, 2 counts.
  • QalqalahقلقلةEcho the letter with a slight bounce.
  • Lam Shamsiyyahلام شمسيةSilent laam, merged into the next letter.
  • Hamzat al-Waslهمزة وصلSkipped when joined to the word before.
  • Madd Tabee'iمد طبيعيNatural madd. Hold 2 counts.

وَشُهُبًاwashuhubaa٨ وَأَنَّاwa'annaa كُنَّاkunnaa نَقْعُدُnaq'udu مِنْهَاminhaa مَقَٰعِدَmaqaa'ida لِلسَّمْعِۖlissam'i فَمَنfaman يَسْتَمِعِyastami'i ٱلْأٓنَl-'na يَجِدْyajid لَهُۥlahoo شِهَابًاshihaaban رَّصَدًاrasadaa٩ وَأَنَّاwa'annaa لَاlaa نَدْرِيٓnadree أَشَرٌّasharrun أُرِيدَureeda بِمَنbiman فِيfee ٱلْأَرْضِl-'ardi أَمْam أَرَادَaraada بِهِمْbihim رَبُّهُمْrabbuhum رَشَدًاrashadaa١٠ وَأَنَّاwa'annaa مِنَّاminnaa ٱلصَّٰلِحُونَs-saalihoona وَمِنَّاwaminnaa دُونَdoona ذَٰلِكَۖdhaalika كُنَّاkunnaa طَرَآئِقَtaraa'iqa قِدَدًاqidadaa١١ وَأَنَّاwa'annaa وَرَسُولَهُۥwarasoolahoo فَإِنَّfa'inna لَهُۥlahoo نَارَnaara جَهَنَّمَjahannama خَٰلِدِينَkhaalideena فِيهَآfeehaa أَبَدًاabadaa٢٣ حَتَّىٰٓhattaa إِذَاidhaa رَأَوْاْra'awaa مَاmaa
