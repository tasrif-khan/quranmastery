Tajweed keyrules in this surah
  • Madd Muttasilمد متصلHold 4 to 5 counts.
  • Madd Munfasilمد منفصلHold 4 to 5 counts.
  • Madd al-Aaridمد عارضHold 2, 4 or 6 counts when stopping.
  • IqlabإقلابNoon turns into a meem sound.
  • Idghaam w/ Ghunnahإدغام بغنةMerge into the next letter with nasalisation.
  • Idghaam w/o Ghunnahإدغام بغير غنةMerge into the next letter, no nasalisation.
  • IkhfaإخفاءHide the noon with a light nasal sound.
  • GhunnahغنةNasal sound, 2 counts.
  • QalqalahقلقلةEcho the letter with a slight bounce.
  • Lam Shamsiyyahلام شمسيةSilent laam, merged into the next letter.
  • Hamzat al-Waslهمزة وصلSkipped when joined to the word before.
  • Madd Tabee'iمد طبيعيNatural madd. Hold 2 counts.
Al-Balad البلد Meccan · 20 ayat
لَآlaa أُقْسِمُuqsimu بِهَٰذَاbihaadhaa ٱلْبَلَدِl-balad١ وَأَنتَwa'anta حِلُّۢhillu بِهَٰذَاbihaadhaa ٱلْبَلَدِl-balad٢ وَوَالِدٍwawaalidin وَمَاwamaa وَلَدَwalad٣ لَقَدْlaqad خَلَقْنَاkhalaqnaa ٱلْإِنسَٰنَl-'insaana فِيfee كَبَدٍkabad٤ أَيَحْسَبُayahsabu أَنan لَّنlan يَقْدِرَyaqdira عَلَيْهِ'alayhi أَحَدٌahad٥ يَقُولُyaqoolu أَهْلَكْتُahlaktu مَالًاmaalan لُّبَدًاlubadaa٦ أَيَحْسَبُayahsabu أَنan لَّمْlam يَرَهُۥٓyarahoo أَحَدٌahad٧ أَلَمْalam نَجْعَلnaj'al لَّهُۥlahoo عَيْنَيْنِ'aynayn٨ وَلِسَانًاwalisaanan وَشَفَتَيْنِwashafatayn٩ وَهَدَيْنَٰهُwahadaynaahu ٱلنَّجْدَيْنِn-najdayn١٠ فَلَاfalaa ٱقْتَحَمَq-tahama ٱلْعَقَبَةَl-'aqabah١١ وَمَآwamaa أَدْرَىٰكَadraaka مَاmaa ٱلْعَقَبَةُl-'aqabah١٢ فَكُّfakku رَقَبَةٍraqabah١٣ أَوْaw إِطْعَٰمٌit'aamun فِيfee يَوْمٍyawmin ذِيdhee مَسْغَبَةٍmasghabah١٤ يَتِيمًاyateeman ذَاdhaa مَقْرَبَةٍmaqrabah١٥ أَوْaw مِسْكِينًاmiskeenan ذَاdhaa مَتْرَبَةٍmatrabah١٦ ثُمَّthumma كَانَkaana مِنَmina ٱلَّذِينَl-ladheena ءَامَنُواْaamanooaa وَتَوَاصَوْاْwatawaasawaa بِٱلصَّبْرِbissabri وَتَوَاصَوْاْwatawaasawaa بِٱلْمَرْحَمَةِbilmarhamah١٧ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَoolaa'ika أَصْحَٰبُashaabu ٱلْمَيْمَنَةِl-maymanah١٨ وَٱلَّذِينَwalladheena كَفَرُواْkafarooaa بِئَايَٰتِنَاbi'aayaatinaa هُمْhum أَصْحَٰبُashaabu ٱلْمَشْئَمَةِl-mash'amah١٩ عَلَيْهِمْ'alayhim نَارٌnaarun مُّؤْصَدَةُۢmu'sadah٢٠
