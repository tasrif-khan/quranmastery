Tajweed keyrules in this surah
  • Madd Laazimمد لازمHold 6 counts. Obligatory.
  • Madd Muttasilمد متصلHold 4 to 5 counts.
  • Madd Munfasilمد منفصلHold 4 to 5 counts.
  • Madd al-Aaridمد عارضHold 2, 4 or 6 counts when stopping.
  • IqlabإقلابNoon turns into a meem sound.
  • Idghaam w/ Ghunnahإدغام بغنةMerge into the next letter with nasalisation.
  • Idghaam w/o Ghunnahإدغام بغير غنةMerge into the next letter, no nasalisation.
  • Idghaam Shafawiإدغام شفويMeem merges into meem.
  • Ikhfa Shafawiإخفاء شفويHide the meem before a baa.
  • IkhfaإخفاءHide the noon with a light nasal sound.
  • GhunnahغنةNasal sound, 2 counts.
  • QalqalahقلقلةEcho the letter with a slight bounce.
  • Lam Shamsiyyahلام شمسيةSilent laam, merged into the next letter.
  • Hamzat al-Waslهمزة وصلSkipped when joined to the word before.
  • Silent letterحرف لا ينطقWritten but not pronounced.
  • Madd Tabee'iمد طبيعيNatural madd. Hold 2 counts.

تَبَارَكَtabaaraka ٱلَّذِيl-ladhee نَزَّلَnazzala ٱلْفُرْقَانَl-furqaana عَلَىٰ'alaa عَبْدِهِۦ'abdihee لِيَكُونَliyakoona لِلْعَٰلَمِينَlil'aalameena نَذِيرًاnadheeraa١ ٱلَّذِيalladhee لَهُۥlahoo إِنَّهُۥinnahoo كَانَkaana غَفُورًاghafooran رَّحِيمًاraheemaa٦ وَقَالُواْwaqaalooaa مَالِmaali هَٰذَاhaadhaa ٱلرَّسُولِr-rasooli يَأْكُلُya'kulu ٱلطَّعَامَt-ta'aama وَيَمْشِيwayamshee فِيfee ٱنظُرْunzur كَيْفَkayfa ضَرَبُواْdarabooaa لَكَlaka ٱلْأَمْثَٰلَl-'amthaala فَضَلُّواْfadallooaa فَلَاfalaa يَسْتَطِيعُونَyastatee'oona سَبِيلًاsabeelaa٩ تَبَارَكَtabaaraka ٱلَّذِيٓl-ladhee إِنin شَآءَshaa'a جَعَلَja'ala لَكَlaka خَيْرًاkhayran مِّنmin ذَٰلِكَdhaalika جَنَّٰتٍjannaatin تَجْرِيtajree مِنmin تَحْتِهَاtahtihaa ٱلْأَنْهَٰرُl-'anhaaru وَيَجْعَلwayaj'al لَّكَlaka كَثِيرًاkatheeraa١٤ قُلْqul أَذَٰلِكَadhaalika خَيْرٌkhayrun أَمْam جَنَّةُjannahu ٱلْخُلْدِl-khuldi ٱلَّتِيl-latee وُعِدَwu'ida ٱلْمُتَّقُونَۚl-muttaqoona كَانَتْkaanat لَهُمْlahum جَزَآءًjazaa'an وَمَصِيرًاwamaseeraa١٥ لَّهُمْllahum فِيهَاfeehaa مَاmaa يَشَآءُونَyashaa'oona خَٰلِدِينَۚkhaalideena كَانَkaana عَلَىٰ'alaa رَبِّكَrabbika وَعْدًاwa'dan مَّسْئُولًاmas'oolaa١٦ وَيَوْمَwayawma يَحْشُرُهُمْyahshuruhum وَمَاwamaa يَعْبُدُونَya'budoona مِنmin دُونِdooni ٱللَّهِl-laahi فَيَقُولُfayaqoolu ءَأَنتُمْa'antum أَضْلَلْتُمْadlaltum عِبَادِي'ibaadee هَٰٓؤُلَآءِhaa'ulaa'i أَمْam نُذِقْهُnudhiqhu عَذَابًا'adhaaban كَبِيرًاkabeeraa١٩ وَمَآwamaa أَرْسَلْنَاarsalnaa قَبْلَكَqablaka مِنَmina ٱلْمُرْسَلِينَl-mursaleena إِلَّآillaa إِنَّهُمْinnahum لَيَأْكُلُونَlaya'kuloona
