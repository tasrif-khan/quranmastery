Tajweed keyrules in this surah
  • Madd Munfasilمد منفصلHold 4 to 5 counts.
  • Madd al-Aaridمد عارضHold 2, 4 or 6 counts when stopping.
  • Idghaam w/o Ghunnahإدغام بغير غنةMerge into the next letter, no nasalisation.
  • IkhfaإخفاءHide the noon with a light nasal sound.
  • GhunnahغنةNasal sound, 2 counts.
  • QalqalahقلقلةEcho the letter with a slight bounce.
  • Lam Shamsiyyahلام شمسيةSilent laam, merged into the next letter.
  • Hamzat al-Waslهمزة وصلSkipped when joined to the word before.
  • Madd Tabee'iمد طبيعيNatural madd. Hold 2 counts.
Al-Qari'ah القارعة Meccan · 11 ayat
ٱلْقَارِعَةُalqaari'ah١ مَاmaa ٱلْقَارِعَةُl-qaari'ah٢ وَمَآwamaa أَدْرَىٰكَadraaka مَاmaa ٱلْقَارِعَةُl-qaari'ah٣ يَوْمَyawma يَكُونُyakoonu ٱلنَّاسُn-naasu كَٱلْفَرَاشِkalfaraashi ٱلْمَبْثُوثِl-mabthooth٤ وَتَكُونُwatakoonu ٱلْجِبَالُl-jibaalu كَٱلْعِهْنِkal'ihni ٱلْمَنفُوشِl-manfoosh٥ فَأَمَّاfa'ammaa مَنman ثَقُلَتْthaqulat مَوَٰزِينُهُۥmawaazeenuhoo٦ فَهُوَfahuwa فِيfee عِيشَةٍ'eeshahin رَّاضِيَةٍraadiyah٧ وَأَمَّاwa'ammaa مَنْman خَفَّتْkhaffat مَوَٰزِينُهُۥmawaazeenuhoo٨ فَأُمُّهُۥfa'ummuhoo هَاوِيَةٌhaawiyah٩ وَمَآwamaa أَدْرَىٰكَadraaka مَاmaa هِيَهْhiyah١٠ نَارٌnaarun حَامِيَةُۢhaamiyah١١
