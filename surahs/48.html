Tajweed keyrules in this surah
  • Madd Laazimمد لازمHold 6 counts. Obligatory.
  • Madd Muttasilمد متصلHold 4 to 5 counts.
  • Madd Munfasilمد منفصلHold 4 to 5 counts.
  • IqlabإقلابNoon turns into a meem sound.
  • Idghaam w/ Ghunnahإدغام بغنةMerge into the next letter with nasalisation.
  • Idghaam w/o Ghunnahإدغام بغير غنةMerge into the next letter, no nasalisation.
  • Idghaam Shafawiإدغام شفويMeem merges into meem.
  • Ikhfa Shafawiإخفاء شفويHide the meem before a baa.
  • IkhfaإخفاءHide the noon with a light nasal sound.
  • GhunnahغنةNasal sound, 2 counts.
  • QalqalahقلقلةEcho the letter with a slight bounce.
  • Lam Shamsiyyahلام شمسيةSilent laam, merged into the next letter.
  • Hamzat al-Waslهمزة وصلSkipped when joined to the word before.
  • Madd Tabee'iمد طبيعيNatural madd. Hold 2 counts.

يَبْتَغُونَyabtaghoona فَضْلًاfadlan مِّنَmina ٱللَّهِl-llaahi وَرِضْوَٰنًاۖwaridwaanan سِيمَاهُمْseemaahum فِيfee وُجُوهِهِمwujoohihim مِّنْmin أَثَرِathari ٱلسُّجُودِۚl-ssujoodi ذَٰلِكَdhaalika مَثَلُهُمْmathaluhum فِيfee ٱلتَّوْرَىٰةِۚl-ttawraahi وَمَثَلُهُمْwamathaluhum فِيfee ٱلْإِنجِيلِl-'injeeli كَزَرْعٍkazar'in أَخْرَجَakhraja شَطْئَهُۥshat'ahoo فَئَازَرَهُۥfa'aazarahoo فَٱسْتَغْلَظَfastaghlaza فَٱسْتَوَىٰfastawaa عَلَىٰ'alaa سُوقِهِۦsooqihee يُعْجِبُyu'jibu ٱلزُّرَّاعَl-zzurraa'a لِيَغِيظَliyagheeza بِهِمُbihimu ٱلْكُفَّارَۗl-kuffaara وَعَدَwa'ada ٱللَّهُl-llaahu ٱلَّذِينَl-ladheena ءَامَنُواْaamanooaa وَعَمِلُواْwa'amilooaa ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِl-ssaalihaati مِنْهُمminhum مَّغْفِرَةًmaghfirahan وَأَجْرًاwa'ajran عَظِيمَۢا'azeemaa٢٩
